- Ring of integers of a pure cubic field (Dedekind; see e.g. the exercises on pure cubic fields in Marcus, Number Fields, Ch. 2): let m be a squarefree integer, m ≠ 0, ±1, let θ be the real cube root of m and K = Q(θ). If m ≢ ±1 (mod 9), then O_K = Z[θ] and d_K = −27m^2. If m ≡ ±1 (mod 9), then O_K has Z-basis θ, θ^2, (1 ± θ + θ^2)/3 (the sign as in m ≡ ±1 mod 9), so [O_K : Z[θ]] = 3, and d_K = −3m^2.
- Dedekind–Kummer theorem (Neukirch, Algebraic Number Theory, Ch. I, Prop. 8.3; Marcus, Number Fields, Thm 27): let K = Q(θ) with θ an algebraic integer whose minimal polynomial is f in Z[x], and let p be a prime not dividing the index [O_K : Z[θ]]. If f ≡ g_1^(e_1) ... g_r^(e_r) (mod p) with g_1, ..., g_r distinct monic polynomials irreducible mod p, then pO_K = P_1^(e_1) ... P_r^(e_r), where P_i = (p, g_i(θ)) are distinct prime ideals of norm p^(deg g_i).
