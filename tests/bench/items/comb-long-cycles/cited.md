- Factorial moments of cycle counts (Arratia, Barbour and Tavaré, Logarithmic Combinatorial Structures, EMS 2003, Chapter 1): for a uniform random permutation of {1, ..., n} and nonnegative integers r_1, ..., r_m with r_1 + 2r_2 + ... + m r_m <= n, E[product over k of (C_k)_(r_k)] = product over k of (1/k)^(r_k); for fixed m, (C_1, ..., C_m) converges in distribution, as n -> infinity, to independent Poisson variables (Z_1, ..., Z_m) with E[Z_k] = 1/k.
- Bonferroni inequalities (e.g. Feller, An Introduction to Probability Theory and Its Applications, Vol. 1, 3rd ed., Ch. IV): for events B_1, ..., B_N and S_j the sum of P(B_(i_1) and ... and B_(i_j)) over i_1 < ... < i_j, the probability that none of the B_i occurs lies between consecutive partial sums of 1 - S_1 + S_2 - S_3 + ...; it is at least 1 - S_1 + ... - S_(2l+1) and at most 1 - S_1 + ... + S_(2l).
