- Eisenstein's criterion (Dummit and Foote, Abstract Algebra, 3rd ed., Section 9.4): if f = x^n + a_(n−1) x^(n−1) + ... + a_0 is in Z[x] and p is a prime dividing every a_i with p^2 not dividing a_0, then f is irreducible over Q.
- Irreducibility of cyclotomic polynomials (Dummit and Foote, Section 13.6, Theorem 41): the n-th cyclotomic polynomial is irreducible over Q of degree φ(n), so [Q(ζ_n) : Q] = φ(n); and Q(ζ_n)/Q is Galois with group isomorphic to (Z/nZ)^× (Section 14.5, Theorem 26).
- Fundamental theorem of Galois theory (Dummit and Foote, Section 14.2, Theorem 14): for a finite Galois extension K/F, an intermediate field E is Galois over F if and only if Gal(K/E) is normal in Gal(K/F); in particular, if Gal(K/F) is abelian, every intermediate field is Galois over F.
- Compositum with a Galois extension (Dummit and Foote, Section 14.4, Proposition 19 and Corollary 20): if K/F is a finite Galois extension and F'/F is any finite extension inside a common field, then KF'/F' is Galois with group isomorphic to Gal(K/K ∩ F'), and [KF' : F] = [K : F] [F' : F] / [K ∩ F' : F].
